About Switch Day-0

A web app for Cisco switch day-0 configuration.

Version 1.0

What is Switch Day-0?

An engineer picks an approved template, fills in the form, and copies the rendered IOS-XE text into the switch CLI. The server does not store the generated configuration.

Drafts stay off the generate page until an approver locks them. Revising a locked template opens a new draft and leaves the locked copy in place.
